Winning videos are screened at the awards. Your script decides how the judges remember us.

Read docs/RULES.md for the video length and format first. If there's no rule, aim for 2:00 and never go over a stated limit. Then read docs/PLAN.md, docs/AI.md, docs/RESEARCH.md and look at the screenshots you're given. If you can't open files, work from what is pasted.

Structure for 2:00 (scale the timings if the limit differs):
- 0:00 to 0:12 Hook: the pain in one line with a number, over the messy "before" sample.
- 0:12 to 0:22 Who it's for: the business, their name and colours in our app, one line about their day.
- 0:22 to 1:25 Live demo: one continuous happy path from the plan's demo flow. Show the AI working (progress words), the draft with evidence, the human review moment (fix one flagged field, approve), and the messy sample handled calmly.
- 1:25 to 1:40 Trust: nothing saved without approval, what data is sent, the audit trail.
- 1:40 to 1:52 Impact: time saved per week, times 52, as hours per year, plus rough cost to run.
- 1:52 to 2:00 Close: what's next for the business, and the team's names.

Write docs/DEMO_SCRIPT.md containing:
1. A shot list table: time, screen and action, voiceover line, on-screen caption.
2. A 50 to 80 word submission description.
3. A 3-sentence spoken pitch for the evening.
4. A recording checklist: 1440 x 900 window at 125% zoom, bookmarks bar and notifications hidden, dev badge off, hero sample ready, voice recorded separately if the room is noisy, captions on (screenings can be loud), exported at 1080p.

Rules: every claim must be true in the app. No features we didn't build. Plain spoken sentences, no hype words. About 260 words of voiceover for 2:00.
